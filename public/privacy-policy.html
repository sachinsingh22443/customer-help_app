Privacy Policy – Eat Unity

Last Updated: June 2026

Eat Unity respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

  • Name
  • Phone Number
  • Email Address (if provided)
  • Delivery Addresses
  • Location Information
  • Order History
  • Subscription Information
  • Payment Information processed through secure third-party payment providers

How We Use Information

  • Provide food ordering services
  • Process and manage orders
  • Display nearby chefs and kitchens
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Provide customer support
  • Send important account and order notifications

Location Data

Location data is used to help users discover nearby chefs, calculate delivery areas, and improve service accuracy.

Payments

Payments are processed through trusted third-party payment providers. Eat Unity does not store complete card or banking details on its servers.

Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures and encrypted connections (HTTPS) to protect user information.

Account Deletion

Users can permanently delete their account directly from the app by navigating to:

Profile → Delete Account

When an account is deleted, personal information associated with the account is permanently removed, except where retention is required by law.

Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services such as payment gateways, cloud hosting, maps, analytics, and notification providers to deliver services.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
